Your corner, in your ears.

Boxing Coach is built for solo boxers who want structured training without staring at a screen. Start the round, put the phone down, and follow real-time combination, movement, defense, bell, and rest cues.

TRAIN FREE

• Basic and Medium coaching levels
• Adjustable rounds, round length, and recovery
• Shadowboxing or heavy-bag sessions based on your equipment
• Optional spoken coaching and on-screen combinations
• Local workout history, streaks, progress, and share cards
• Post-workout feedback that tunes your next session
• No account required to train

PROGRESS WITH PREMIUM

Premium is an optional one-time lifetime purchase. It unlocks:

• 42 structured sessions across three progressive programs
• Beginner Fundamentals, Heavy Bag Conditioning, and Fight Camp
• Advanced and Pro coaching levels
• Purchase and restore through your App Store account—no Boxing Coach account required

BUILT FOR CONSISTENCY

After each workout, tell Boxing Coach whether it felt too easy, just right, or too hard. Your next standard workout changes one variable at a time, while program sessions advance or repeat at your pace. Set reminders only when you choose, and keep your workout history on your device.

Boxing Coach does not measure punch power, technique, or heart rate. Punch totals count issued coaching calls, and calories are estimates. Train within your ability, use suitable space and equipment, and consult a qualified professional before beginning a new exercise program when appropriate.

The localized one-time Premium price and final purchase details are shown by the App Store before confirmation. There are no recurring charges.
